YT Empire Painel

A private, read-only analytics panel used by the owner of a small group of YouTube channels to follow the performance of his own channels.

What it does

  • Reads public and owner-authorized statistics of the owner's own YouTube channels (views, likes, comments, subscribers, watch time, audience retention) through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API.
  • Shows these numbers, day by day, in a panel that only the channel owner uses.
  • Uses read-only access. It never uploads, edits, deletes or comments on anything, and never posts on the owner's behalf.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the channels. It is an internal tool, not a public service: there are no sign-ups and no other users.

Google sign-in

The owner signs in once with Google to authorize read-only access to his channels (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly and, optionally, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly). Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Em português

O YT Empire Painel é um painel interno, pessoal e somente leitura que o dono de um pequeno grupo de canais do YouTube usa para acompanhar os números dos próprios canais.

  • Lê, com autorização do dono, as estatísticas dos canais dele (views, likes, comentários, inscritos, tempo de exibição, retenção) pela YouTube Data API e pela YouTube Analytics API.
  • Acesso só de leitura: nunca envia, edita, apaga ou comenta nada.
  • Só o dono dos canais usa. Não é um serviço público e não tem cadastro de usuários.
  • Revogar acesso: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
